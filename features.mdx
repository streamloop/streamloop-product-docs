Streamloop turns pre-recorded videos and audio into a continuous live stream that runs 24/7 on
YouTube or any RTMP platform. Everything runs in the cloud: there's no OBS to set up, no encoding
hardware and no PC left switched on.

This page covers what Streamloop can do. To put it to work, start with the [quickstart](/quickstart).

## Two kinds of loop

| | Pre-recorded video | Scene (private beta) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Your uploaded videos on repeat | A live show you design in the studio |
| Maximum quality | 4K · 60 fps, with HDR | 1080p · 60 fps |
| Playlists, background music, smart order | Yes | Yes |
| Live inputs (RTMP, SRT, RTSP, HLS) | No | Yes |
| Web pages, live data, overlays, transitions | No | Yes |
| AI assistant that runs the studio | No | Yes |

Both kinds stream 24/7 from the cloud, manage your YouTube channel, run on start and stop schedules,
and can use a backup stream. See [Scenes](/scenes/overview).

## Stream destinations

- **YouTube**: connect your channel through the official YouTube API, and Streamloop creates and
  manages the live broadcast for you. Streamloop meets YouTube's
  [live-streaming verification requirements](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6259859).
  You can also paste a stream key from YouTube Studio instead. See [YouTube](/platform/youtube).
- **Other platforms**: Twitch, Facebook, Kick, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, or any platform that accepts an
  RTMP stream URL and key. See [Twitch](/platform/twitch) and
  [Other RTMP platforms](/platform/other-rtmp).
- **Several at once**: a loop can stream to up to 5 destinations from one encode (at most one of
  them YouTube). Multistream is rolling out. See [Multistream](/platform/multistream).

## Video quality

- **Resolution**: 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K (2160p) for video loops. Scene loops go up to 1080p.
- **Frame rate**: 24, 25, 30 or 60fps.
- **HDR**: stream high dynamic range video where your destination and your viewers' devices
  support it.

Higher resolution and frame rate cost more per hour. See [Billing](/billing).

## Playlists

- **Queue multiple videos** in a single loop and **drag to reorder** them.
- **Choose how it loops**: *In order*, *Shuffle* (no repeats until every video has played), or a
  [*Smart order*](/smart-order) you describe in plain words, such as an intro first, a jingle every
  few songs, or the news at the top of every hour.
- **Edit while you're live**: add, remove or reorder videos on a running stream, then publish the
  change. The on-air playlist updates with no stop and restart.

See [Build and schedule your playlist](/build-and-schedule-your-playlist).

## Your video and audio

- **Common formats**: upload MP4, MOV or MKV video, and MP3, WAV, FLAC or AAC audio. Each file can
  be up to 1.5 GB on a free account or 5 GB once you've paid.
- **Import from a link**: pull media from **Google Drive**, **YouTube** or a direct download link.
  Streamloop fetches it on its servers at the quality you choose, so you can close the tab while it
  works.
- **Separate audio track**: play one track or a whole audio playlist over your video, for music,
  radio and ambient channels.
- **Library**: find, preview, rename and remove everything you've uploaded. See [Library](/library).

## Scheduling

- **Schedule a start**: set a future date and time, and the stream goes live on its own.
- **Stop automatically**: give a loop a run length or an end time, or let it run indefinitely.
- **Repeat**: run the same window every day, or on chosen days every week.
- **Start when uploads finish**: go live as soon as your pending uploads are added.

## Team access

Every account has a workspace. Invite teammates to it, and everyone works on the same loops,
uploads and destinations with their own sign-in. See [Workspaces and team](/workspaces-and-team).

## Reliability and security

- **Always-on uptime**: Streamloop runs on distributed cloud infrastructure and maintains **over
  99.9% uptime**. It watches every stream and restarts it automatically if it stalls or drops.
- **Encrypted credentials**: your YouTube authorization and stream keys are encrypted at rest.

See [Reliability](/reliability-guarantees).

## YouTube broadcast controls

When you connect a YouTube channel, Streamloop sets up and runs the broadcast for you:

- Set the stream **title, description, category, language and tags**.
- Choose **visibility**: Public, Unlisted or Private.
- Pick a **thumbnail**: automatic (the first frame of your video) or a custom image.
- Toggle **recording** and **DVR**, and choose a **latency** mode (normal, low or ultra-low).
- **Schedule on YouTube**: when you schedule a loop, Streamloop creates the upcoming YouTube
  broadcast and keeps it in sync if you reschedule or cancel.

See [YouTube](/platform/youtube) for the full setup.

## FAQ
