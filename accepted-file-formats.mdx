Streamloop accepts common video containers including **MP4, MOV, MKV, and AVI**, with codecs such
as **H.264, H.265/HEVC, and AV1**. Common audio formats include **MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, and Opus**.

## File-size limits

- **Free users:** up to **1.5 GB per file**
- **Paid users:** up to **5 GB per file**

You can split a larger video into multiple chunks and add the chunks to one playlist.

## Destination compatibility

Use **H.264** when streaming to non-YouTube destinations such as Twitch for the broadest
compatibility. YouTube also accepts modern codecs such as H.265/HEVC and AV1.
