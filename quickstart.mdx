This guide takes you from a video file to a live 24/7 stream. It takes a few minutes.

<Info>
  You need a **video file** and a **destination** to stream to: a YouTube channel, or a stream key
  from Twitch, Facebook, Kick, X, TikTok or any other RTMP platform.
</Info>

A *loop* is a stream you set up once and leave running. There are two kinds:

- **Pre-recorded video** loops play your uploaded videos on repeat, at up to 4K 60fps. This guide
  covers them.
- **Scene** loops are live shows you design in the studio, with live inputs, web pages and
  overlays. Scenes are in private beta. See [Scenes](/scenes/overview).

## Your first stream

When you sign up at [streamloop.app](https://streamloop.app), Streamloop walks you through your
first loop step by step. You pick the type, a name and a destination, connect the destination, pick
the quality, add your media and choose how to pay. The steps below follow the same path from the
dashboard, for your next loops.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a loop">
    On the **Loops** page, click **New loop**. Enter a **Loop name** (for example `Nature sounds`)
    and pick a **Resolution** and **Framerate** (24, 30 or 60 fps). If you see a choice between
    **Pre-recorded video** and **Scene**, click **Choose video**. Otherwise click **Create loop**.
    The loop opens on its **Playlist** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your video">
    On **Video**, click **Upload** to add a file (MP4, MOV or MKV, up to 1.5 GB on a free account
    or 5 GB once you've paid), or **From URL** to import from Google Drive, YouTube or a direct
    link. Add more videos to build a playlist, or add an **Audio** track to play over the video.
    See [Build and schedule your playlist](/build-and-schedule-your-playlist).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a destination">
    Open the **General** tab. Under **Destination**, click **Connect a destination**. Pick
    **YouTube · Connect channel** to link your channel with Google, or pick a platform (or
    **Custom RTMP**) and paste its stream key. See [YouTube](/platform/youtube),
    [Twitch](/platform/twitch) and [Other RTMP platforms](/platform/other-rtmp).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quality">
    Still in **General**, **Output quality** shows the resolution (720p, 1080p, 1440p or 2160p (4K))
    and frame rate (24, 25, 30 or 60 fps). Higher quality costs more per hour, and 1080p at 30fps
    suits most streams. Streamloop also streams HDR video when your destination supports it. See
    [Billing](/billing) for prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    When the loop shows **Stream is idle**, click **Start stream**. Your video starts looping to
    your destination within a minute or two. To go live later, turn on **Schedule loop**, enter a
    time like `tomorrow 9am`, and click **Schedule the stream**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your loop is live. It keeps streaming 24/7 from the cloud. You can close your laptop and it stays
  on air.
</Check>

<Note>
  Streaming uses credits from your balance. Eligible new accounts can claim free credits to try
  Streamloop. You can also start a monthly plan with a \$1 trial, or top up as you go. See
  [Billing](/billing).
</Note>
