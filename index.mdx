Welcome to the official documentation for **Streamloop** ([streamloop.app](https://streamloop.app)),
the cloud tool that turns pre-recorded videos and audio into a continuous **24/7 live stream**.
Stream at up to 4K 60fps with HDR, send one loop to several platforms, work with your team, and
close your laptop after you go live.

New here? The [quickstart](/quickstart) takes you from a video file to a live stream in a few
minutes.
