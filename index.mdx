Welcome to the official documentation for **Streamloop** ([streamloop.app](https://streamloop.app))
— the cloud tool that loops pre-recorded videos and audio into a continuous **24/7 live stream**.
Stream in HDR at up to 4K 60fps, work with your team, and close your laptop after you go live.

New here? The [quickstart](/quickstart) takes you from a video file to a live stream in a few
minutes.
