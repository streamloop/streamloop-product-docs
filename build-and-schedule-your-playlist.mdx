A video loop plays a **playlist** (one video or many) to your destinations on repeat. You can
reorder the videos, shuffle them, add a separate audio track, schedule or repeat the loop, and
change the playlist while you're streaming.

## Add your videos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Playlist tab">
    In your loop, open the **Playlist** tab and select **Video**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add videos">
    Pick one of these:

    - **Upload** a file from your computer, or drag files onto the list. MP4, MOV and MKV work.
      Each file can be up to **1.5 GB** on a free account or **5 GB** once you've paid. Split
      longer videos into parts.
    - **From URL** imports from Google Drive, a YouTube link or a direct download link. Streamloop
      fetches the file on its servers, so you can close the tab while it works. A YouTube playlist
      link lists its videos and you confirm which ones to import. For each video you choose a
      quality, and for YouTube links you can import just a section.
    - **From library** reuses a file you already uploaded, without uploading it again. See
      [Library](/library).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder them">
    Drag any video to change its position. The order you see is the order it plays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Uploads that aren't in any playlist are removed automatically after 14 days.
</Note>

## Choose how it loops

The **Loop behavior** section sets the order each time the playlist repeats:

- **In order**: play 1 → 2 → 3, then repeat.
- **Shuffle**: random order, with no repeats until every video has played.
- **Smart order**: describe the order in plain words ("an intro first, then the rest", "the news at
  the top of every hour") and an AI assistant builds it. See [Smart order](/smart-order).

## Add a separate audio track

Switch to **Audio** to add audio that plays *over* your video instead of its original sound. This
suits music, radio and lofi channels. Audio can be one track or a whole playlist, with its own
loop behavior.

<Tip>
  Building a music or lofi channel? See [Build a 24/7 music or lofi channel](/lofi-music-channel).
</Tip>

## Schedule when it goes live

The loop's status panel has the **Schedule** and **Expiration** controls:

- **Schedule loop**: turn this on and type a start time in plain words, like `in 2h` or
  `tomorrow 9am`, then click **Schedule the stream**. The loop shows **Scheduled to start** and goes
  live on its own. Before then you can **Start now** or **Cancel schedule**.
- **Loop expiration**: set a run length or an end time, and the stream stops on its own. A run
  length (like 2 hours) starts counting when the stream goes live, not when you set it.
- **Start when uploads finish**: the loop goes live as soon as your pending uploads are added.

### Repeat a schedule

To run the same time window again and again, set **Recurrence** to **Daily** or **Weekly**:

1. Pick **Daily**, or pick **Weekly** and then the days of the week.
2. Enter a **From** and **To** time. Times use your local time zone.
3. Click **Schedule**.

The window must be shorter than 24 hours, and it can end after midnight. Each run is a normal start
and stop. On a [managed YouTube connection](/platform/youtube), each run gets its own YouTube
broadcast.

While a repeating loop runs, **Stop for today** ends the current run and keeps the next one
scheduled. **Cancel schedule** also turns repeat off. If a run can't start (for example, the
destination needs reconnecting), the loop shows **Repeat is paused until this is fixed**. Fix the
problem, then click **Schedule again**.

## Edit the playlist while you're live

You don't have to stop a running stream to change what's playing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    While the stream is live, add, remove or reorder videos in the **Playlist** tab as usual.
    Nothing changes on air yet. Your edits are a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **View changes** to review what's pending, then **Publish playlist**. The on-air version
    (shown as **Live (v1)**, **Live (v2)** and so on) updates without a restart. If a publish fails,
    click **Retry publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Resolution and frame rate changes need a restart. While a loop is live or scheduled, you can stop
  sending to one of several destinations, but you can't add one. See
  [Multistream](/platform/multistream).
</Note>
