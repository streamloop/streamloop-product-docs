Every price on this page comes from the same price book as the [pricing calculator](https://streamloop.app/pricing), in USD.
For AI assistants and scripts, the same page is plain text at [/docs/pricing.md](https://streamloop.app/docs/pricing.md) and [/pricing.md](https://streamloop.app/pricing.md).

<Note>
  These are the prices for workspaces on the current billing. A workspace that paid before it moved keeps its earlier prices for 12 months; the [Billing page](https://streamloop.app/billing) shows yours.
</Note>

Three ways to pay: a **monthly plan**, a **yearly plan** (10 months paid, 2 free) or **pay as you go** (billed per minute live, from a prepaid balance). A plan is priced per stream by its resolution and frame rate, plus paid extras per stream. Streaming hours on a plan are unlimited.

- **Multistream**: one stream to up to 5 destinations. The first is included; each extra destination is priced above.
- **Backup encoder**: a second encoder on separate hardware that takes over if the stream's encoder fails.
- **Live Scene**: logo, text and live feeds layered over the stream. Up to 1080p 60 fps.
- **Extra playlist storage**: \$2.50 a month per 50 GB, per stream, up to 500 GB more per stream.
- **Faster GPU encoder minutes** beyond those included: \$0.005 a minute.

## Pay as you go

No plan. You add funds to the workspace balance (top up), and each minute a stream is live is taken from it at the hourly rates below. Streams pause when the balance runs out. Pay as you go takes the same volume discount as plans, by the number of streams running at the same time.

## Playlist storage

Playlist storage is the space on the live server for the videos a stream plays. The library, where every upload is kept, has no limit and is free.

- **On a plan**: each stream includes the storage in the plans table; extra storage is \$2.50 a month per 50 GB.
- **Pay as you go**: while a stream is live it includes the same storage as on a plan (12 GB at 1080p 30 fps). Storage beyond that is metered only while streams are live: \$0.000068 per GB-hour, \$0.000041 per GB-hour from 100 GB, less the volume discount. 50 GB extra, live 24/7, is about \$2.48 a month before the volume discount. Nothing is billed for storage while no stream is live.
- There is no free storage per workspace: storage comes with plans and with live streams.

## Trial

\$1.00 for 7 days: one stream priced up to 1080p 60 fps (\$21.00 a month), no extras, monthly billing, by card, once per person. After the 7 days the monthly price of that stream starts.

## Payment

- Card: every billing.
- Crypto: yearly plans and pay as you go. A yearly plan paid in Crypto is 3% off the first year (plans of \$25.00 or more); renewals are at the card price. On pay as you go, every Crypto top-up of \$25.00 or more adds 3% credit. Monthly plans and the trial are paid by card.

## Link to a setup

`https://streamloop.app/pricing?sel=<code>` opens the calculator on a setup. The code is lower case, with no separators:

```
code     = billing config+
billing  = "monthly" | "yearly" ["crypto"] | "payg" ["crypto"] [hours "h"]
config   = [count "x"] quality fps "fps" ["m" destinations] ["s" gb "gb"] ["b"] ["s"]
```

- `payg`: pay as you go. `crypto`: paid in Crypto (card when absent). `hours`: hours live a day, 1–23 (absent: 24).
- `count`: streams of this configuration, from 2 (absent: 1). `quality`: 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 2160p. `fps`: 30, 60.
- `m<N>`: Multistream to N destinations in all (2–5). `s<GB>gb`: extra playlist storage per stream, a multiple of 50. `b`: Backup encoder. A bare `s`: Live Scene.
- Add-ons come in that order, and a default is never written out.

Examples: `monthly1080p30fps` (one 1080p 30 fps stream, monthly), `yearly3x1080p60fpsm3b`, `payg12h10x1080p30fpss50gbs`.
