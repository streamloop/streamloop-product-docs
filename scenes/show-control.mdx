Once your scene loop is live, you run the show from the studio's **Live** panel. Open it from the
left sidebar.

## What's on air

The top of the **Live** panel shows the scene on air, and the next scene if one is cued. If it says
**Stream offline**, the loop isn't running: start it with **Go live** or from the loop's page.

## Switch scenes

Under **Cut to**, click a scene to take it to air. Streamloop plays the scene's **transition**: a
cut, fade, slide, wipe or dip. You can also right-click a scene on the canvas and choose **Go live**.

## Set controls

**Controls** are switches and values you set while the show runs, for example a "Breaking news"
toggle, a guest's name in a lower third, or a button that starts a countdown. They appear under
**Controls** in the **Live** panel. A change shows on air right away.

Add controls while building the show (or ask the [AI assistant](/scenes/ai-assistant)), then
publish.

## Watch your sources

Under **Sources**, each source shows its status (**Live**, **Waiting**, **Down** and so on), and a
playlist shows the track that's playing. A live input that's down shows its fallback on air. See
[Live inputs](/scenes/live-inputs#when-a-feed-drops).

## Update data

Under **Tables**, **Send to air** pushes a table's current rows to the running show at once, without
waiting for the next refresh.

## Publish while live

Edits in the studio are a draft until you click **Publish**. While the loop is live, publishing swaps
in the new version without stopping the stream. Viewers see the change, but the broadcast never
drops.

## Ask the assistant

The [AI assistant](/scenes/ai-assistant) can run the show too: take a scene to air, cue the next
scene so the switch is instant, skip a playlist to its next track, set a control or start an ad
break. It asks for your approval before each of these.

## Automate it

A show can switch scenes on its own: on a schedule you set (for example, a "Starting soon" scene
before the hour), when data changes, or when a control flips. Ask the assistant to set this up, or
write a script in **Scripts**.
