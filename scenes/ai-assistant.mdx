The studio's **Assistant** builds and edits your show from plain-language requests. Ask for "a
lo-fi radio frame with the current track and a clock" or "a scoreboard from this sheet", and it
creates the frames, layers, tables and playlists.

## Start with the assistant

A new, empty show opens on the assistant's prompt:

1. Describe the show you want, or pick one of the starter ideas.
2. Attach references if you have them: drop or paste pictures, logos, a brand sheet or data files.
3. Send. The **Assistant** panel opens on the right and shows each step as it works.

You can open the assistant at any time from the **Assistant** tab in the right panel.

## What it can do

- **Build and edit** frames, layers, components, scripts and controls.
- **Bring in data**: try a URL to see what it returns, and turn it into a [table](/scenes/data-and-sheets).
- **Fill playlists** with your media.
- **Search the web** for feeds, pages and facts to use in your show.
- **Check its work** by looking at a picture of the frame, and line it up against a reference you
  attached.
- **Ask you** when a request is unclear and a wrong guess would cost real work, with a few options
  to pick from.

Each change the assistant makes is one undo step.

## It asks before going on air

Anything that changes what viewers see right now needs your approval first: taking a frame to air,
starting an ad break, and similar actions. The assistant shows what it wants to do, and you allow or
deny it. Edits to the draft don't reach viewers until you [publish](/scenes/build-a-scene#publish).

## Privacy

- The assistant never sees your [secrets](/scenes/data-and-sheets#keep-keys-secret) or camera
  passwords.
- Chats are saved per scene for each person, so you can pick up where you left off, up to 50 chats
  per scene.

## Limits

The assistant has a daily usage limit per workspace. If you reach it, try again the next day (UTC).
