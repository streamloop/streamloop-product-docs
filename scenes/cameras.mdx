An **IP camera** source pulls a camera's live feed straight from the internet over **RTSP**, the
protocol most IP cameras speak. Streamloop connects to the camera, so nothing needs to run on your
side.

## Before you start

- **The camera must be reachable from the internet**: a public IP address, or a port forward on your
  router (RTSP usually uses port 554). Cameras on a private or local network address are refused.
- If your camera can push RTMP itself, use an [RTMP input](/scenes/live-inputs) instead.
- You need the camera's RTSP address, for example `rtsp://203.0.113.7:554/stream1`, and its
  username and password if it has one. The camera's manual or app lists the address.

## Add a camera

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the source">
    In **Sources & layers**, click **Add source** and pick **IP camera**. Drag it onto a frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the **RTSP URL**, **Username** and **Password**. Leave **Transport** on **TCP** unless your
    camera only works over UDP. TCP gets through most routers and firewalls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test connection**. A working camera shows its resolution, frame rate and a snapshot, which
    the canvas uses as its preview. Cameras that send H.265 video work, but show no snapshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save camera**, then [publish](/scenes/build-a-scene#publish) the scene.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the test fails

| Result | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Refused | The address points to a private network. Use the camera's public address or a port forward. |
| Unreachable | The camera or router isn't accepting connections. Check the port forward and that the camera is on. |
| Timeout | The camera didn't answer in time. Check the address and port. |
| Auth | The username or password is wrong. |
| Not found | The address is right but the stream path isn't. Check the camera's manual for its RTSP path. |
| Unsupported | The camera sends a format Streamloop can't play. Try another stream profile on the camera. |

## Keep it working

- **Passwords stay private.** The camera's password is encrypted and never shown again, not even to
  your teammates or the AI assistant. The field shows **Saved (blank keeps it)**: leave it blank to
  keep the saved password.
- **Dynamic DNS.** If your camera uses a dynamic DNS name and its address changes, open the camera
  and click **Save camera** again so Streamloop picks up the new address.
- **When the camera drops**, the layer follows its **If it drops** setting, such as a "Camera
  reconnecting" slate. See [Live inputs](/scenes/live-inputs#when-a-feed-drops).
