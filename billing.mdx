Streamloop runs on **credits**. You spend them only while a stream is live, and they never expire.
Buy credits as you go, or choose an optional monthly plan that adds credits every month. Credits
belong to your [workspace](/workspaces-and-team), so everyone in it streams from the same balance.

## What you pay for

All charges are metered, so you pay only for what you use:

1. **Streaming time**: the time your loop is on air. This is the main cost.
2. **Video preparation**: a one-time fee of about **\$0.005 per minute of encoding time** to
   convert each video into a stream-ready format. You're charged only when the conversion succeeds.
3. **Options you turn on**: extra destinations, a backup stream, extra storage and scene rendering.
   Each is described below.

### Streaming time

The rate depends on the **resolution** and **frame rate** of the loop. You're billed by the minute
while you're live. The figures below are the cost of a stream running **nonstop for 30 days**:

| Resolution  | 24 fps   | 25 fps   | 30 fps   | 60 fps   |
| ----------- | -------- | -------- | -------- | -------- |
| 720p        | \$4.00   | \$4.17   | \$5.00   | \$7.50   |
| 1080p       | \$8.00   | \$8.33   | \$10.00  | \$15.00  |
| 1440p       | \$16.00  | \$16.67  | \$20.00  | \$30.00  |
| 4K (2160p)  | \$24.00  | \$25.00  | \$30.00  | \$45.00  |

A stream you run for a week costs about a quarter of these figures, and one you run for a day about
a thirtieth. For example, 1080p at 30fps costs about **\$0.0139 per hour**, so about \$0.33 for a
day.

<Accordion title="Exact hourly rates in credits">
  **\$1 = 1,000,000 credits.** Each hour live costs the rate below. Monthly figures are 720 hours
  (30 days).

  | Resolution | 24 fps | 25 fps | 30 fps | 60 fps |
  | ---------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
  | 720p       | 5,556  | 5,792  | 6,944  | 10,417 |
  | 1080p      | 11,111 | 11,569 | 13,889 | 20,833 |
  | 1440p      | 22,222 | 23,153 | 27,778 | 41,667 |
  | 4K (2160p) | 33,333 | 34,722 | 41,667 | 62,500 |

  Video preparation is **5,000 credits (\$0.005) per minute of encoding time**.
</Accordion>

### Extra destinations

A loop can stream to [up to 5 destinations](/platform/multistream). The first is included. Each
extra destination that's turned on adds a flat fee, by resolution:

| Resolution | Per extra destination, per 30 days |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 720p       | \$3.50                             |
| 1080p      | \$6.00                             |
| 1440p      | \$8.00                             |
| 4K (2160p) | \$12.50                            |

Like streaming time, it's charged only while the loop is live.

### Backup stream

On a YouTube destination you can turn on **Backup stream**. Streamloop then publishes to YouTube's
primary and backup ingest at the same time. This **doubles your streaming cost** while live.

### Extra storage

Every loop includes storage for playlists up to **10 GiB**. A bigger playlist needs **Extra
storage**, which you turn on in the loop's **General** tab, under **Storage**. Only the part above
10 GiB is charged, at **\$0.50 per GiB-month**, and only while the loop is live. The change applies
the next time the stream starts.

<Note>
  Extra storage is rolling out. If your loop has no **Storage** section, it isn't available in your
  workspace yet.
</Note>

### Scenes

[Scene loops](/scenes/overview) pay the normal streaming rate plus a **scene rendering** charge.
During the beta, scene rendering costs 30% less than the launch price. At the default beta price, a
1080p 30fps scene loop costs **\$20 for 30 days**: \$10 of streaming plus \$10 of scene rendering.

| Scene rendering, per 30 days | 24 fps | 30 fps | 60 fps  |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------- |
| 720p                         | \$3.56 | \$4.44 | \$8.89  |
| 1080p                        | \$8.00 | \$10.00 | \$20.00 |

Scenes stream at up to 1080p60. The dashboard shows your exact price when you create a scene loop.

## Free credits

Eligible new accounts can claim **free credits** with **no card required**. Use them to test your
setup, your destination and the quality before you spend anything. Free credits aren't refundable.

## Ways to pay

### Monthly plan (optional)

A monthly plan adds a fixed amount of credits to your balance every month, at a better rate than
topping up. You can pick one during onboarding or from **Billing**.

- **\$1 trial**: a \$1 card charge saves your card and starts a 7-day trial. The \$1 is added to
  your balance as credits. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing more.
- After the trial, your card is charged monthly and the full amount lands in your balance as
  credits.
- Cancel any time from **Billing**. Unused credits stay yours.

### Top up as you go

- Top up from **\$5** to **\$10,000** at a time.
- Pay by card (through Stripe or Keepz) or with crypto (USDC, ETH, BTC and more).
- Larger top-ups get bonus credit: **+3%** on \$250 or more, **+5%** on \$500 or more, and **+2%
  extra** when you pay with crypto.

### Auto-refill

**Auto-refill** tops up from your default card whenever your balance falls below an amount you set,
so loops never stop mid-stream. It isn't available while a monthly plan is active, because the plan
already adds credits every month.

## If you run low or out of credits

- **Starting a stream** checks your balance first. If you don't have enough credits for the loop
  you've set up, it won't start until you top up.
- **While you're live**, credits drain by the minute. If your balance runs out, your loops **stop
  automatically**, so you're never charged beyond what you've bought. Your videos and settings stay
  put. Top up and start again whenever you like.

<Tip>
  A loop's **Costs** panel shows its hourly rate and a monthly estimate, including extra
  destinations and storage, so you can top up before a 24/7 stream runs dry.
</Tip>

## Invoices and refunds

- Download each invoice as a PDF from **Billing**. Add a company name, tax ID and address under
  billing details, and they appear on your invoices, including past ones.
- You can request a refund on **unused credits from a purchase** from the matching invoice. A
  refund is capped at the credits you have left, and it goes back to your original payment method.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do credits expire?">
    No. Credits stay on your account until you use them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a subscription?">
    No. The monthly plan is optional. You can top up only when you need credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does a month of 24/7 streaming cost?">
    About \$4 (720p), \$8 (1080p), \$16 (1440p) or \$24 (4K) per 30 days at 24fps, for one
    destination. Higher frame rates, extra destinations, a backup stream and scenes cost more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I run out of credits mid-stream?">
    Your loops stop automatically. Nothing else is affected: your videos and settings remain, and
    you can restart once you top up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
